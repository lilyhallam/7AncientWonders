The Hanging Gardens of Babylon

  • Little is known about the gardens, however it is thought that they were located near the royal palace in Babylon (now modern day Iraq) and built between 800 - 560 BC.
  • A popular theory is that the gardens were built within the walls of the royal palace, were on the roofs of ziggurat terraces and watered by an advanced pump system fed by the nearby Euphrates River.
  • However, research from the late 20th and early 21st centuries suggests that the gardens were constructed in a different ancient city called Nineveh, and were laid out in a sloping pattern, similar to a mountain.
