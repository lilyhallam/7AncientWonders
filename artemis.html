The Temple of Artemis

  • The Temple of Artemis was built in the ancient city of Ephesus which now lies in modern day western Turkey.
  • The temple was dedicated to the goddess Artemis and was first built in around 550 BC but was burned down in 356 BC by a madman named Herostratus, however, it was rebuilt after this.
  • It was famous for its large size as well as the level of decorative artworks that covered it.
  • However, in the year 262, the temple was destroyed by invading Goths and wasn't rebuilt.
  • There are fragments of the structure that remain and are held in the British Museum.
