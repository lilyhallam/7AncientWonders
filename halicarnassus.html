The Mausoleum of Halicarnassus

  • The Mausoleum of Halicarnassus is the elaborate tomb of Mausolus, the ruler of Caria (south-west Turkey), and was built between 353 - 351 BC in the capital city of Halicarnassus.
  • The structure was a tall, almost square podium that was heavily decorated on all four sides and topped with a round of columns and stepped roof.
  • Some other decorations include a four horse chariot and free-standing statues on the podium.
  • It is likely that the structure was destroyed by an earthquake between the 11th - 15th century.
  • The term ‘mausoleum’ is now widely used for many monumental tombs.
