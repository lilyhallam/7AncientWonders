The Statue of Zeus at Olympia

  • The Statue of Zeus was a large statue of the Greek god, Zeus who was the god of the sky as well as the ruler and protector of all gods and humans.
  • It was held in the large Temple of Zeus at Olympia in western Greece and was created by the sculptor Phidias in around 430 BC.
  • The statue stood for 1,000 years and was worshiped as the representation of Zeus, becoming the most famous and important statue in Greece.
  • However, the temple was destroyed in 426 AD and the statue was either destroyed then, or in a fire around 50 years later.
