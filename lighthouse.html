The Lighthouse of Alexandria

  • The lighthouse was built in around 280 BC and stood on the small island of Pharos in the harbour of Alexandria.
  • Ptolemy I ordered the construction of the lighthouse, which took 30 years to build and was finished during the reign of his son, Ptolemy II.
  • The lighthouse was a technical triumph and the second tallest man made structure after the Pyramids of Giza. It became the blueprint for all lighthouses made after it.
  • It was destroyed by an earthquake in the 1300s, many of the blocks used to construct it fell into the sea where they were discovered in 1994.
